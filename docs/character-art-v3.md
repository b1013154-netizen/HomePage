# CHARACTER FORGE — アニメ調ポートレート

制作方法: 内蔵 image_gen ツール。実行時の画像生成はせず、事前制作した24種類を選択します。

素材: `public/assets/character-anime-atlas-v3.png`。1024 × 1536、4列 × 6行、各256 × 256。画像の加工はせず、CSSの背景位置で各ポートレートを表示します。

識別順は左上から横方向。世界観・職業で候補を絞り、シードで選択。利用者が一覧から固定指定することもできます。保存・JSONには `visual.animeIndex` と `visual.artAsset` を含みます。画像は設定のイメージであり、すべての装備・外見記述を再現するものではありません。

## 使用した生成プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production sprite atlas of 24 original anime character portrait icons for a Japanese game character generator. Create ONE clean image with an EXACT uniform 4-column by 6-row grid, 24 equally sized SQUARE cells, edge-to-edge with NO gutters, NO borders, NO rounded corners, NO text, no numbers, no watermarks. Entire canvas aspect ratio 2:3 portrait. Each cell contains exactly ONE different adult character, head and shoulders, centered face, complete hair with generous safety space, shoulders in lower quarter. Consistent polished Japanese RPG anime art: expressive detailed eyes, delicate sharp linework, layered flowing hair, cel shading with painterly light, tasteful rich colors, no simplistic geometric faces, not chibi. Soft dark color gradients behind each subject. Clearly distinct faces, skin tones, hair lengths and ages, adult women and men, modest genre clothing. Exact row order left to right: Row1 fantasy: silver-haired woman knight in blue armor, dark-haired man knight in bronze armor, violet-haired woman mage with star ornament, long white-haired adult man mage in blue robes. Row2 eastern: black-haired woman samurai with red hair ribbon, dark-haired man ronin, shrine mystic woman with gold hairpin, mature man Chinese scholar in jade robes. Row3 everyday: auburn-haired woman baker in apron, friendly middle-aged man shopkeeper in waistcoat, adult woman academic in glasses and navy blazer, young adult man detective in gray coat. Row4 technology: cyan-haired woman cybernetic hacker, dark-skinned man space pilot, red-haired woman steampunk engineer with goggles, silver-haired man researcher in futuristic jacket. Row5 gothic and myth: pale woman occult scholar in modest black lace collar, mature bearded man inquisitor, white-haired woman oracle in gold and ivory garments, elegant dark-haired man arcane noble with burgundy cravat. Row6 survival and adventure: short-haired woman wasteland scout with olive scarf, rugged older man scavenger, tan-skinned woman airship navigator in teal travel coat, brown-haired man adventurer in worn green cloak. Every face unique, all human. No weapons crossing cell boundaries. Consistent scale and perfectly aligned equal cells; this atlas will be sliced by CSS, so exact grid is essential.
